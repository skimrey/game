Face Sheet — NPC faces for review

Every NPC's silly face is generated. This sheet shows a batch of them (3D look on the left, the dialog portrait on the right), the mayor and the inn's regulars first, then villagers sorted by oddness (characters/npcs.js ODDNESS — each quirk scores; a face may spend at most ODDNESS_BUDGET). Features that read as unsettling in review (clown nose, pointy ears, cyclops and spiral eyes, red lips, fangs, warts) were dropped. Faces at the budget are outlined. Add ?seed=N for another batch; ?people=N shows the folk you'd meet together on town visit N (mayor, regulars, card battlers, tamers — 3D bust, dialog portrait, 3D full figure); ?parts=1 shows each face feature alone.
